Pitch Overview

The Problem

Small businesses in emerging markets lose up to 8% of revenue to high fees and slow processing times when making international payments. Traditional banking solutions are inaccessible to 60% of these businesses.

Our Solution

NexaPay provides a mobile-first payment platform with instant settlements, 70% lower fees, and no minimum balance requirements. Our blockchain-backed infrastructure ensures security and transparency.

Target Market

SMBs and freelancers in Southeast Asia and Africa conducting cross-border transactions. Initial focus on India-UAE and Nigeria-UK corridors representing $45B annual volume.

Unique Value Proposition

The only platform combining instant settlements, local currency payouts, and WhatsApp integration for businesses without smartphone apps. 3x faster onboarding than competitors.
